NOAA MONTHLY CLIMATE
Climate data provided by NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory, Boulder, Colorado,
USA. NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis 1 monthly 1991-2020 long-term means.
https://www.psl.noaa.gov/data/help/

These public-domain federal data are climatology, not live weather, a forecast,
airport observations or an official NOAA product. Altitude lapse adjustments,
biome labels, cloud-layer geometry and snow/ice visual cues are modeled. Cloud
fraction itself is an interpolated monthly reanalysis field.
