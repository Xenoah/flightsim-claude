Electronic Arts fastnoise texture provenance

The bundled src/bluenoise/stbn.ktx2 consists of 32 RGB layers whose decoded bytes match the Electronic Arts fastnoise vector3_uniform_box3x3_exp0101_product_0.png through _31.png, in numeric order and without a vertical flip. This is a format conversion of the supplied EA textures; it is not attributed to NVIDIA STBN.
