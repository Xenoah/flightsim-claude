Portions of the zune-jpeg entropy bitstream and Huffman table routines are adapted from libjpeg-turbo / Independent JPEG Group code. They were translated from C into Rust and modified, including byte-reader and marker handling, bit-buffer representation, bounds and error checks, and lookahead/AC-table integration. The exact libjpeg-turbo revision originally consulted is not stated in the zune source; the matching primary reference preserved with this review is libjpeg-turbo 2.1.4, commit 8162eddf041e0be26f5c671bb6528723c55fed9d, jdhuff.c.
